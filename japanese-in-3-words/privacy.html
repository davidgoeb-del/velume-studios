Privacy Policy

Learn Japanese in 3 Words (三語)
Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. No Data Collection

Learn Japanese in 3 Words does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data.

2. On-Device Storage Only

Your learning progress, level selection, and saved bookmarks are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app removes this local data.

3. Offline & Ad-Free

All lessons and audio files are bundled inside the app. There are no accounts, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no advertisements.
