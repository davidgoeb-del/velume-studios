The 3-Beat Method (三語)

Spoken Japanese is learned fastest in natural 2–3 beat chunks rather than isolated vocabulary. Every phrase breaks down into clickable beats with kanji, kana, romaji, and literal meaning so learners internalize syntax in context.

Register & Cultural Context

Cards explicitly tag Polite / Social, Casual / Peer, and Universal register alongside real-world cultural notes—preventing textbook stiffness or accidental rudeness.

Dual Native Audio & Active Recall

Every item features natural-speed and slow-speed native recordings (Hiro & Miiko), paired with a 4-stage progression (Learn → Read → Listen → Recall), Focus Deck bookmarking, and a full Hiragana/Katakana chart & quiz.
