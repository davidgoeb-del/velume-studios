Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

Velume Studios ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile application, Lumora, and our website.

1. Data Collection

We believe in privacy by design. Lumora does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not track your personal identity.

2. Local Storage

Any data generated while using Lumora (such as your progress, selected language, or saved vocabulary/expressions) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data. If you delete the app, this local data will be deleted as well.

3. Third-Party Services

Lumora is a standalone application and does not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs that harvest your personal data.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our app does not collect personal data, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@velumestudios.com.
