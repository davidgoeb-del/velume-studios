Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Velume Studios ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile applications, including Velume Matte and Lumora, and our website (velumestudios.com).

1. Data Collection

We believe in privacy by design. Our applications do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, photos, or images on our servers. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not track your personal identity. All photo processing, editing, and rendering occurs 100% locally on your device.

2. Local Storage

Any settings or data generated while using our apps are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your photos or personal files. If you delete the app, this local data will be deleted as well.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications do not integrate with third-party tracking, advertising, or data-harvesting SDKs. In-app purchases are securely processed by Apple via the App Store and RevenueCat solely for verifying subscription or lifetime purchase entitlements.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our apps do not collect personal data or transmit photos, they are safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@velumestudios.com.
