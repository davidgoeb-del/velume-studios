Lumora

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 9, 2026 | Velume Studios

1. Overview

At Lumora (operated by Velume Studios), your privacy is fundamental to our core values. Lumora is designed to provide a calm, peaceful learning environment without intrusive tracking, ads, or third-party data monetization.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

Lumora stores your app preferences, selected language, saved phrases, and learning progress directly on your local device using standard iOS device storage (localStorage / Preferences).

  • We do not create user tracking profiles.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use invasive third-party advertising SDKs.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscription Processing

In-App Purchases and subscription entitlements are securely processed via Apple’s App Store In-App Purchase infrastructure and RevenueCat. When you purchase an annual subscription to Lumora, transaction metadata is handled securely by Apple and RevenueCat solely to validate and restore your subscription status. No financial or credit card information is ever stored or accessed by Velume Studios.

4. Audio & System Permissions

Lumora requires standard audio playback capabilities to play studio audio lessons and ambient background lo-fi music. Lumora does not record audio or access your microphone.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be published directly on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Velume Studios at hello@velumestudios.com.
