The Manifesto

Social grids are loud. They squeeze our perspectives, force unnatural crops, and strip away the space our photographs need to breathe.

We believe in preserving the uncropped, absolute truth of a visual composition. We believe in quiet margins, fine-art proportions, and zero nonsense.

We wanted a simple, uncompromised way to let a photograph breathe. So we built Velume Matte.

Just wrap it in a matte and let it speak.

— David Goeb, Velume Studios
