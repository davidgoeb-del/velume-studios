Love & Let Go

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 | Velume Studios

1. Overview

At Love & Let Go (operated by Velume Studios), your privacy is sacred. We believe sentimental items and memories are deeply personal. The App is built from the ground up with a privacy-first architecture: no accounts, no tracking, no advertisements, and zero third-party data collection.

2. Photos & Camera Privacy (100% On-Device)

Your photos never leave your device. All image selection, polaroid frame rendering, and text compositing are executed entirely locally in your device's memory using HTML5 Canvas APIs.

  • We do not upload, host, transmit, or inspect your photos on any server.
  • Camera and Photo Library permissions are requested solely to allow you to capture or choose photos for your keepsakes.
  • Exported keepsakes are saved directly to your Apple Photos library or shared via the native iOS Share Sheet at your discretion.

3. Data Storage & In-App Purchases

Love & Let Go does not require account registration or collect personal identifying information.

  • Local Storage: Your preferences (such as selected frame style and purchase unlock status) are stored locally on your device via standard iOS storage.
  • In-App Purchases: All transactions are processed securely through Apple's App Store in-app purchase infrastructure. Velume Studios does not collect or store your billing information or credit card details.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

Love & Let Go does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or data broker integrations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at hello@velumestudios.com.
